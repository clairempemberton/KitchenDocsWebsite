Identify by plate

Photograph the nameplate, get the docs

Every commercial unit carries a stamped plate with the make, model and serial. Point the camera at it and the app reads it, matches the equipment, and pulls in its manuals, which is also how a new kitchen gets inventoried in an afternoon.

  1. Find the plate. The app has location hints per unit type, because half of them are behind the bottom grill.
  2. Take the photo. Make, model and serial come off the plate.
  3. The unit joins your kitchen with its manuals attached.

Maintenance

The manual already told you when. It just never reminded you.

Grease the bowl-lift every six months. Pull the water filter monthly. Check the door gasket weekly. Every manual carries its service intervals, and every kitchen loses them the day the binder goes in a drawer.

  1. It reads the intervals out of each manual: the schedule is extracted from the book, and every item carries the page it was found on.
  2. You review and accept. Nothing lands on your schedule that you didn't approve, and nothing is proposed that the manual doesn't say.
  3. Tasks sort themselves into overdue, today, this week and later, and the phone reminds you when one comes due.
  4. Mark it done and it reschedules to the next interval. No spreadsheet, no clipboard, no wall calendar going soft above the sink.

Why it exists

“The information was never missing. It was just unreadable at the moment anyone needed it.”

Smart Kitchen Docs comes from the team behind Smart EV Guides, a tool built by a working firefighter so responders could get manufacturer rescue guidance without digging through a 40-page PDF on scene.
